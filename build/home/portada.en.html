Why a new-build developer needs it.

  • You sell before you build.

    While there are only plans, a home you can walk through convinces more than a drawing. And the bank wants to see sales before it finances the build.

  • You reach buyers abroad.

    Many of your buyers live in another country and decide from a distance. The link travels by WhatsApp and email.

  • Your agencies all show the same thing.

    Every agent shares the same link, with your brand and availability up to date. Nobody shows an old version.

A sales tool for your sales team.

  • In the sales officeYour sales team presents every home on a tablet, with up-to-date availability and price.
  • In remote salesThe same link reaches the international buyer by WhatsApp or email.
  • At fairs and agenciesA QR code on the stand or in the brochure opens the whole development.
